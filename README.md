# Motor-Driven Wheelchair Swing

**Project Level:** Intermediate Undergraduate

**Institution:** Middlesex University London  
**Degree:** BEng (Hons) Biomedical Engineering  
**Module:** PDE2401 (Design Engineering Projects 2)  
**Academic Year:** 2nd (2023/2024)  
**Assessment Outcome:** Upper Second Class (Merit)  
**Overall Module Mark:** 5 (Upper Second Class, Merit)
**Project Type:** Concept-stage engineering design and SOLIDWORKS CAD modelling project

## Project Overview

Independently developed a conceptual motor-driven wheelchair swing intended to improve accessibility in recreational playgrounds while reducing the physical effort required of caregivers.

The proposed design incorporated a mechanism for converting rotational motion into oscillation, an electrically operated wheelchair lifting system and sensor-actuated clamps for wheelchair fixation. Additional features included a touchscreen control interface, emergency-stop provision and restricted-access enclosure.

The project followed a structured Engineering Design Process (EDP), progressing from research and product specification to alternative concept development, technical evaluation and final design visualisation using SOLIDWORKS. The project remained at the concept stage, without physical construction or testing.

## Contributions
- Conducted market research and critically analysed existing wheelchair swing designs to identify limitations and opportunities for improvement.
- Developed a Product Design Specification (PDS) informed by wheelchair dimensions, accessibility requirements, relevant standards and technical constraints.
- Generated and comparatively evaluated alternative mechanical solutions, refining initial concepts into the selected design.
- Developed SOLIDWORKS models and technical illustrations to represent mechanical components, operating principles and the overall design.
- Investigated preliminary motor requirements, transmission mechanisms and electrical system considerations to support design decisions.
- Produced a comprehensive technical report and maintained a dated engineering logbook documenting research, calculations, design development and critical reflections.

## Skills Demonstrated

- **Engineering Design Process:** Requirements definition, concept generation, comparative evaluation and design refinement.
- **Computer-Aided Design:** SOLIDWORKS part modelling, mechanical assembly development and three-dimensional visualisation.
- **Mechanical Engineering:** Motion conversion, transmission mechanisms, mechanical actuation and preliminary engineering calculations.
- **Accessibility-Focused Design:** Consideration of wheelchair users, caregiver requirements, inclusive environments and safety constraints.
- **Critical Thinking and Technical Documentation:** Evaluating design trade-offs, identifying limitations and communicating engineering decisions through technical reports and structured logbook development.
